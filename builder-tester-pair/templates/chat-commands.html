Commands for ${builder.name} and ${tester.name} (send them in your Teams self-chat or a Teams DM; only your own messages count)

${tester.name} (product owner + tester)

  • help-${derived.tester_lc} (or ${derived.tester_lc} help): this list
  • ${derived.tester_lc} <new work or a change>: ${tester.name} agrees the details with you, writes the acceptance criteria and design, and hands them to ${builder.name} to build. Example: ${derived.tester_lc} TICKET-123: refuse payments over the account limit
  • ${derived.tester_lc} <question>: ask anything about requirements or testing. Example: ${derived.tester_lc} what did the last retest find?
  • ${derived.tester_lc} take TICKET-123: ${tester.name} picks up that ticket as product owner (same as labelling it ${jira.tester_label})
  • ${derived.tester_lc} restart: a fresh copy of ${tester.name} in about 2 minutes (send it once; repeats while it restarts are ignored)

${builder.name} (builder)

  • help-${derived.builder_lc} (or ${derived.builder_lc} help, or just help): this list
  • ${derived.builder_lc} <what you want>: ask ${builder.name} anything. New work is forwarded to ${tester.name} for a spec first.
  • ${derived.builder_lc} take TICKET-123: ${builder.name} forwards that ticket to ${tester.name}, then builds it once the spec is ready (same as labelling it ${jira.builder_label})
  • review <hand-over id>: before deploying, get the PR link, diff, ${tester.name}'s local result, deploy and rollback commands. Example: review ho-20261012-3f2a1c
  • approve <namespace> <minutes>: open a deploy window for ${builder.name} (max 120 min; the message must be under 15 min old; used once). Example: approve my-namespace 30
  • deployed <hand-over id>: tell ${builder.name} you deployed it yourself, so ${tester.name} retests live
  • close it: reply this when ${builder.name} asks to close a ticket; nothing goes to Done without it
  • ${derived.builder_lc} restart: a fresh copy of ${builder.name} in about 2 minutes (send it once; repeats while it restarts are ignored)

Both: status, answer <hand-over id> <text>, stop / start (stop or resume picking up new work).

Merges and secrets always need you directly. Every hand-over step is posted to the team's change channel.
